Context help pages for the taxes list and form (``account.tax``), shown by
web_context_help.

The pages explain how to set up the tax codes. With the Italian localization
(``l10n_it``) they explain, step by step, how to load:

* **taxes with VAT**: the ordinary rates 22%, 10%, 5% and 4%
* **taxes without VAT**: excluded, not taxable, exempt and out of scope
  operations, with the nature codes N1, N2, N3, N4, N5 and N7 and the law
  reference
* **split payment** (art. 17-ter DPR 633/1972): the group taxes of the
  *Split Payment* fiscal position
* **reverse charge**: sales taxes with the nature codes N6.x and purchase taxes
  with the two distribution lines, and the self-invoice sent to the SdI

Every page follows the standard layout of web_context_help:

* **Contents**: the sections of the page, built by the help window
* **Description**: what taxes are and how they are computed
* **How to insert a new record**: step-by-step guide to create a tax, with a
  guided tour
* **Suggestions for search** (list): menu, search, filters and groupings
* **How to update a record**
* **How to archive or delete record** (form and list)
* **How to set up**: default taxes, fiscal positions and the four Italian
  chapters above
* **Report and export file**
* **Example** button and **FAQ** button, and **Footer**

Pages are in English and Italian, with the fields and options of Odoo 18.0.

How to update a record
----------------------

#. Open the tax from the list and change it: it is saved automatically when
   you leave it, or at once with the cloud icon next to the breadcrumb.
#. The changes apply to the new documents: the posted invoices keep the tax
   amounts they have. Do not change the rate of a tax already used: archive it
   and create a new one.
#. To change the accounts or the tax grids, edit the tables **Distribution for
   Invoices** and **Distribution for Refunds** of the tab **Definition**: they
   must have the same lines, in the same order.
#. To create a similar tax, e.g. *21%* from *22%*, press the gear icon ‣
   **Duplicate** and change the name, the amount and the label.

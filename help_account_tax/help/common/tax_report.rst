.. _report:

Report and export file
----------------------

Taxes have no printable report of their own; they are printed on the
documents that use them:

* The invoice PDF shows the :field:`label on invoices <invoice_label>` of the
  taxes, the :field:`legal notes <invoice_legal_notes>` of the taxes used and
  the totals grouped by :field:`tax group <tax_group_id>`.
* The Italian VAT is reported in the tax report of the accounting, through the
  *Tax Grids* of the distribution lines of the taxes.
* To get a file of the taxes, select them in the list view, then **Actions ‣
  Export**: choose the fields and the format (xlsx or csv). See *Import and
  Export data* in the **Ready** page of the help.

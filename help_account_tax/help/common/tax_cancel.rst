How to archive or delete record
-------------------------------

#. A tax no longer used is archived: it is no longer proposed on the
   documents, but the old invoices keep it. In the form, or in the list, click
   the **Active** toggle to switch it off.
#. The list shows the archived taxes in grey; the filter **Inactive** shows
   only them. To restore a tax click its **Active** toggle again.
#. To delete a tax, open it and use the gear icon ‣ **Delete**, or select the
   taxes in the list and use **Actions ‣ Delete**. Odoo does not forbid it, but
   a tax already used by invoices, by a fiscal position or by the default
   taxes of the company would disappear from them: archive it instead. A
   tax that is the child of a group of taxes, e.g. the split payment ones,
   must not be deleted alone.

.. _example:

Example
-------

**Find the reverse charge taxes**

Type ``RC`` in the search bar: the list shows e.g. *22% S RC* and *0% RC
N6.3*. The ones archived by the fiscal localization are in
grey: click the **Active** toggle to use them.

**Find the taxes of a nature**

Type the nature in the search bar, e.g. ``N6.3``: the tax names of the Italian
package have the nature at the end (*22% RC N6.3*). The nature itself is a
field of the form, see *How to insert a
tax without VAT*.

How to insert a new record by duplicate
---------------------------------------

When the tax you need is not in the package, the quickest way is to copy the
most similar one: the copy keeps the distribution lines, the accounts and the
tax grids.

#. Open the tax most similar to the one you need (same type, scope and nature)
   and press the gear icon ‣ **Duplicate**: the copy keeps the distribution
   lines, the accounts and the tax grids.
#. Change the :field:`tax name <name>` (the copy is called *<name> (Copy)*),
   the :field:`amount <amount>`, the :field:`label on invoices
   <invoice_label>` and the :field:`tax group <tax_group_id>`.
#. Check :field:`Tax Type <type_tax_use>`, :field:`Tax Scope <tax_scope>` and
   :field:`Country <country_id>`, and leave the form to save it.

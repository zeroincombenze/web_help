Editing a page
--------------

Change the :field:`Content <html>` to adapt the page to your company.
An edited page keeps your version when the module is upgraded: if the source
changes, a warning asks to **reset to source** or to **keep your version**.

The help editors of level **User** change the pages of priority lower than
64 only. On a page of priority 64 or more, e.g. a page of a module, they
press **Edit a copy**: the copy gets the default priority (32), so it is
shown in place of the original, and the original stays as it is. The
editors of level **System** change every page.

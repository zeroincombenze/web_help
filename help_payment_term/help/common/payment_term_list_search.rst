How to search
-------------

* **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣**
  :version:`<13|**Management**;*|**Invoicing**` **‣ Payment Terms** lists
  the payment terms.
* Type in the search bar to search the terms by name, e.g. ``30`` finds
  *30 Days* and *30 Days End of Month*.
* :version:`<13|The archived terms, no longer used, are found with **Filters
  ‣ Add Custom Filter**, *Active* is not set.;*|**Filters ‣ Archived** shows
  the terms no longer used.` *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition, e.g.
  on the company.
* **Group By** groups the terms, e.g. by company in a multi-company
  database\ :version:`<17|, through *Add Custom Group*`.

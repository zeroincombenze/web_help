How to archive or delete record
-------------------------------

#. Payment terms no longer used are archived: they are no longer proposed,
   but the old invoices keep them. :version:`<12|In the form press [\`Edit\`],
   untick *Active* and press [\`Save\`];12|In the form press [\`Active\`] at the
   top right;<17|In the form use **Action ‣ Archive**;*|In the form use the
   gear icon ‣ **Archive**`, or select the terms in the list and use
   :version:`<17|**Action ‣ Archive**;*|**Actions ‣ Archive**`.
#. :version:`<13|Archived terms are found with a custom filter on *Active*
   not set;*|The *Archived* filter shows the archived terms`;
   :version:`<12|open one, press [\`Edit\`], tick *Active* and press [\`Save\`] to
   restore it;12|open one and press [\`Archived\`] to restore it;<17|select
   them and use **Action ‣ Unarchive** to restore them;*|select them and use
   **Actions ‣ Unarchive** to restore them`.
#. To delete payment terms, open them and use :version:`<17|**Action ‣
   Delete**;*|the gear icon ‣ **Delete**`, or select them in the list and
   use :version:`<17|**Action ‣ Delete**;*|**Actions ‣ Delete**`.
   :version:`<13|Terms already used can be deleted too, but the invoices
   using them lose them: archive them instead.;*|Terms used by invoices
   cannot be deleted: archive them instead.`

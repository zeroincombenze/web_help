Update record
-------------

#. Open the payment terms from the list.
   :version:`<17|Press **Edit**, change them and press **Save**.;*|Change
   them: they are saved automatically.`
#. To change a line, :version:`<16|click it to open its form;*|click the
   value in the table`; to add an instalment press
   :version:`<12|**Add an item**;*|**Add a line**`; to remove a line click its
   bin icon.
#. Invoices already :version:`<13|validated;*|posted` keep their due dates:
   the changes apply to the new invoices. To keep the old rule for the
   existing documents, create new terms instead of changing them.
#. To create similar terms, e.g. *60 Days* from *30 Days*, press
   :version:`<17|**Action ‣ Duplicate**;*|the gear icon ‣ **Duplicate**` and
   change the name and the days.
#. Terms no longer used are archived with
   :version:`<17|**Action ‣ Archive**;*|the gear icon ‣ **Archive**`: they are
   no longer proposed, but the old invoices keep them. Terms never used can be
   deleted with :version:`<17|**Action ‣ Delete**;*|the gear icon ‣
   **Delete**`.

.. _options:

Options
-------

* **Filters**: *My Quotations* and *My Orders* show your documents only;
  *Quotations*, *Sales Orders*, *To Invoice* and *To Upsell* narrow the list.
* **Group By**: salesperson, customer or order date, with subtotals.
* :field:`Invoice status <invoice_status>`: *Nothing to Invoice*,
  *To Invoice*, *Fully Invoiced* or *Upselling Opportunity*.
* Optional columns are shown or hidden from the menu at the right of the
  column headers.

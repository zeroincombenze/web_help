.. _search:

Suggestions for search
----------------------

* **Sales ‣ Orders ‣ Quotations** lists the quotations, **Sales ‣ Orders ‣
  Orders** the confirmed orders, **Sales ‣** :version:`11|**Invoicing**;*|**To
  Invoice**` **‣ Orders to Invoice** the orders waiting for an invoice.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Order* (number or
  customer reference), *Customer*, *Salesperson* or :version:`11|*Sales
  Channel*;*|*Sales Team*`.
* **Filters**: *My Orders* shows your documents only;
  :version:`11|*Quotations*, *Quotations Sent*, *Sales*, *To Invoice* and
  *Upselling*;*|*My Quotations*, *Quotations*, *Sales Orders*, *To Invoice*
  and *To Upsell*` narrow the list; *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: *Salesperson*, *Customer* or :version:`11|*Order Month*;*|*Order
  Date*`, with subtotals; :field:`invoice status <invoice_status>` (*Nothing to
  Invoice*, *To Invoice*, *Fully Invoiced*, *Upselling Opportunity*) can be
  used in a custom filter.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.

Configuration
-------------

Open **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**:

* **Products**: product variants and units of measure.
* **Pricing**: *Sale Price*, a single price per product, prices per customer
  segment (pricelists) or advanced formulas.
* **Quotations & Sales**: *Customer Addresses*, *Discount* on lines,
  *Sections* on orders, *Online Quotations* (templates), default terms and
  conditions, *Sale Order Modification* (lock confirmed orders), warnings,
  taxes shown on lines, margins.
* **Invoicing**: the default *Invoicing Policy*, ordered or delivered
  quantities, which each product can override in its **Invoicing Policy**
  field, and the down payment product.

Payment terms are managed in **Accounting ‣ Configuration ‣ Payment Terms**,
sales teams in **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Sales Teams**.

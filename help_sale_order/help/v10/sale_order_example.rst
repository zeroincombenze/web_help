.. _example:

Example
-------

**30% down payment before production**

#. Create the quotation and press **Confirm Sale** when the customer accepts.
#. Press **Create Invoice**, choose *Down payment (percentage)*, type 30 and
   press **Create and View Invoices**: validate the invoice and send it.
#. When the goods are delivered press **Create Invoice** again with
   *Invoiceable lines (deduct down payments)*.

**The customer wants 2 more units after the confirmation**

#. Open the order, press **Edit** and raise the quantity of the line from 10 to
   12, then **Save**.
#. Inventory creates the procurement for the 2 more units; the invoice status
   becomes *To Invoice* again.

**Wrong product on a confirmed order**

Set the quantity of the wrong line to 0 and add a line with the right product.

**The customer refuses the quotation**

Press **Cancel**; the quotation stays in the list with status *Cancelled*.

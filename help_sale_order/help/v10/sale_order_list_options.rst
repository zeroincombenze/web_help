.. _options:

Options
-------

* **Filters**: *My Orders* shows your documents only; *Quotations*,
  *Quotations Sent*, *Sales*, *To Invoice* and *Upselling* narrow the list.
* **Group By**: salesperson, customer or order month, with subtotals.
* :field:`Invoice status <invoice_status>`: *Nothing to Invoice*,
  *To Invoice*, *Fully Invoiced* or *Upselling Opportunity*.

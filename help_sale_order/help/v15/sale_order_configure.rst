.. _configure:

How to set up
-------------

Open **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**:

* **Product Catalog**: *Variants*, *Units of Measure*\ :version:`>=13|,
  *Product Configurator* and *Sales Grid Entry*`.
* **Pricing**: *Discounts* on the lines, :version:`11-12|*Multiple Sales
  Prices per Product* (pricelists)`\ :version:`>=13|*Pricelists*, simple or
  advanced`, *Margins*.
* **Quotations & Orders**: :version:`11|*Quotations Templates*;*|*Quotation
  Templates*`, :version:`>=12|*Default Quotation Validity*, *Online Signature*
  and *Online Payment*,` *Customer Addresses*, :version:`11|*Lock Confirmed
  Orders*;*|*Lock Confirmed Sales*` (a confirmed order can no longer be
  changed), :version:`11|*Warnings*;*|*Sale Order Warnings*`, *Pro-Forma
  Invoice*.
* **Invoicing**: the default *Invoicing Policy*, ordered or delivered
  quantities, which each product can override in its **Invoicing Policy**
  field, and the down payment product.

Payment terms are managed in **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣**
:version:`<13|**Management**;*|**Invoicing**` **‣ Payment Terms**, sales teams
in **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Sales Teams**.

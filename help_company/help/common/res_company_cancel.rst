How to archive or delete record
-------------------------------

#. A company no longer used is archived: open it and use the gear icon ‣
   **Archive**, or select the companies in the list and use **Actions ‣
   Archive**. Its branches are archived too. A company that is still the
   default company of an active user cannot be archived: change the default
   company of the user first.
#. **Filters ‣ Archived** in the list shows the archived companies; select
   them and use **Actions ‣ Unarchive** to restore them.
#. To delete a company, open it and use the gear icon ‣ **Delete**, or select
   it in the list and use **Actions ‣ Delete**. A company used by users,
   documents or journal entries cannot be deleted: archive it instead.

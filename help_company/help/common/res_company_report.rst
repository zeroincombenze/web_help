.. _report:

Report and export file
----------------------

Companies have no printable report of their own: their name, logo, address
and Tax ID are printed in the header and footer of every document.

* **Settings ‣ General Settings ‣ Companies ‣ Configure Document Layout**
  sets layout, fonts, colors, header and footer of the reports, with a
  preview.
* To get a file of the companies, select them in the list view, then
  **Actions ‣ Export**: choose the fields and the format (xlsx or csv). See
  *Import and Export data* in the **Ready** page of the help.

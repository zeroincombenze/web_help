How to insert a new record
--------------------------

#. Open **Settings ‣ Users & Companies ‣ Companies** and press **New**
   (administrators only). A branch is created instead from the **Branches**
   tab of its main company.
#. Type the :field:`company name <name>` (required) and click the image to
   load the logo.
#. In the **General Information** tab fill in the address, choosing the
   :field:`country <country_id>` first: it sets the address format and the
   fields of the local laws (e.g. Codice Fiscale and Regime fiscale for
   Italy).
#. Fill in the :field:`Tax ID <vat>`, the :field:`Company ID
   <company_registry>`, the :field:`currency <currency_id>`, phone, email
   and website.
#. The record is saved automatically when you leave it; press the cloud icon
   next to the breadcrumb to save at once.
#. Allow the users to work for the new company: open **Settings ‣ Users &
   Companies ‣ Users** and add the company to their *Allowed Companies*.
#. With Invoicing installed, switch to the new company in the top bar, open
   **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings** and choose the *Fiscal
   Localization* (e.g. *Italy*) to load chart of accounts and taxes.

How to update a record
----------------------

#. Open the company from **Settings ‣ Users ‣ Companies**, or the current
   company from **Settings ‣ General Settings ‣ Configure your company
   data**. Press **Edit**, change the fields and press **Save**.
#. Address, Tax ID, phone and email are stored on the partner of the
   company: the change is shown at once on the new documents; documents
   already printed keep the old data.
#. The :field:`currency <currency_id>` cannot be changed once the company
   has journal items.
#. To put the company in a group, set its :field:`Parent Company
   <parent_id>` (only with *Manage multiple companies*).
#. A company cannot be archived in this version; a company not used by any
   document can be deleted from the list.

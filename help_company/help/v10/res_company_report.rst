.. _report:

Report and export file
----------------------

Companies have no printable report of their own: their logo, Company
Tagline, name, address and Tax ID are printed in the header and footer of
every document.

* **Settings ‣ General Settings**, section **Report Configuration**, sets
  the paper format and the footer of the reports: check *Custom footer* to
  write the footer by hand.
* To get a file of the companies, select them in the list view, then
  **Action ‣ Export**: choose the fields and the format (Excel or CSV). See
  *Import and Export data* in the **Ready** page of the help.

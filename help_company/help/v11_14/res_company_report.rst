.. _report:

Report and export file
----------------------

Companies have no printable report of their own: their logo, name, address
and Tax ID are printed in the header and footer of every document.

* **Settings ‣ General Settings**, section **Business Documents**, sets the
  paper *Format* and the *Document Template* of the reports: **Change
  Document Template** chooses another layout.
* To get a file of the companies, select them in the list view, then
  **Action ‣ Export**: choose the fields and the format (xlsx or csv). See
  *Import and Export data* in the **Ready** page of the help.

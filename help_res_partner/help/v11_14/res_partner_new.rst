How to insert a new record
--------------------------

#. Open **Contacts**, or **Sales ‣ Orders ‣ Customers**, or the vendors menu
   of the purchase app, and press **Create**.
#. Choose **Company** or **Individual** and type the :field:`name <name>`
   (required). For a person of a company, type the company in the
   :field:`company <parent_id>` field: the address is taken from the company.
#. Fill in the address, the :field:`TIN <vat>`, phone, mobile and
   :field:`email <email>`, the :field:`tags <category_id>` and the
   :field:`language <lang>`.
#. In the **Contacts & Addresses** tab of a company press **Create** to add a
   person or an address: choose its type (contact, invoice, delivery or other
   address), then fill in its data and press **Save & Close**.
#. In the :version:`<=12|**Sales & Purchases**;*|**Sales & Purchase**` tab
   choose the :field:`salesperson <user_id>`\ :version:`<=12| and check
   **Is a Customer** and/or **Is a Vendor** (set automatically by the menu you
   started from)`.
#. With Invoicing installed, complete the **Invoicing** tab: payment terms,
   fiscal position and bank accounts.
#. Press **Save**.

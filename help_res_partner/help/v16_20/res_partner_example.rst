.. _example:

Example
-------

**The customer wants invoices sent to its head office**

Open the company, tab **Contacts & Addresses**, press **Add**, choose
*Invoice Address* and type the head office address. Sales orders of the
company propose it as invoice address.

**A new employee of a customer**

Open the company, tab **Contacts & Addresses**, press **Add**, keep the type
*Contact* and type name, job position, email and phone: the address is the
company's one.

**The same customer was created twice**

Open the list view, select both contacts, **Actions ‣ Merge**, keep the most
complete one and press **Merge Contacts**.

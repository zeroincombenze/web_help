.. _update_record:

Update record
-------------

#. Open the contact from **Contacts** and change the fields; the record is
   saved when you leave it, or press the cloud icon. Changes of the tracked
   fields are logged in the chatter.
#. The Tax ID and the commercial data (payment terms, pricelist, fiscal
   position, …) of a company are shared by all its contacts and addresses,
   and its address is copied to its people of type *Contact*: change them on
   the company.
#. Documents already created keep the address they were made with; new
   documents use the new data.
#. **Actions ‣ Archive** hides a contact no longer used without losing its
   history; the *Archived* filter shows it again and **Actions ‣ Unarchive**
   restores it.
#. To merge duplicated contacts select them in the list view, then **Actions
   ‣ Merge**: choose the contact to keep; the documents of the others move to
   it and the others are deleted.
#. To delete a contact select it and use **Actions ‣ Delete**: a contact used
   by documents cannot be deleted, archive it instead.

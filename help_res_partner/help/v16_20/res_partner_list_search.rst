.. _search:

Suggestions for search
----------------------

.. help-version:: 18

   .. help-tour:: res_partner_search
      :label: Show me how to search contacts

      .. help-step:: filters
         :click:

         Press the arrow at the right of the search bar: it opens the
         **Filters**, **Group By** and **Favorites** menus.

      .. help-step:: css:.o_filter_menu
         :position: auto

         Choose *Companies* to list the companies only (*Individuals* for the
         persons). The filter is shown in the search bar; its ✕ removes it.

      .. help-step:: css:.o_add_custom_filter
         :position: auto

         **Add Custom Filter** builds a condition on any field, e.g. *City*
         contains *Milan*: press it to open the condition editor.

      .. help-step:: search
         :position: bottom

         Or type in the search bar, e.g. the name of a company, and choose the
         field to search in: *Search Related Company for: …* lists the people
         of that company; ▸ next to it picks the exact company.

* **Contacts** lists all the contacts; **Sales ‣ Orders ‣ Customers**,
  **Purchase ‣ Orders ‣ Vendors** and the **Customers** / **Vendors** menus of
  Invoicing show the same contacts.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Name* (also the company
  name), *Related Company*, *Email*, *Phone* (phone or mobile), *Tag* or
  *Salesperson*. Several criteria are combined; remove one with its ✕.
* **Filters**: *Individuals* and *Companies*; *Archived* shows the archived
  contacts; *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: *Salesperson*, *Company* or *Country*; *Add Custom Group*
  groups by any field, e.g. tags or city.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.
* In the list view optional columns (mobile, street, Tax ID, tags, …) are
  shown or hidden from the menu at the right of the column headers.

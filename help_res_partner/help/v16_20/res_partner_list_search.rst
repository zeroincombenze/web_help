.. _search:

How to search
-------------

* **Contacts** lists all the contacts; **Sales ‣ Orders ‣ Customers**,
  **Purchase ‣ Orders ‣ Vendors** and the **Customers** / **Vendors** menus of
  Invoicing show the same contacts.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Name* (also the company
  name), *Related Company*, *Email*, *Phone* (phone or mobile), *Tag* or
  *Salesperson*. Several criteria are combined; remove one with its ✕.
* **Filters**: *Individuals* and *Companies*; *Archived* shows the archived
  contacts; *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: *Salesperson*, *Company* or *Country*; *Add Custom Group*
  groups by any field, e.g. tags or city.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.
* In the list view optional columns (mobile, street, Tax ID, tags, …) are
  shown or hidden from the menu at the right of the column headers.

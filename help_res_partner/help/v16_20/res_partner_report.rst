.. _report:

Report
------

* There is no PDF report of a contact. The smart buttons at the top of the
  form, added by the installed apps (*Meetings*, *Sales*, *Purchases*,
  *Invoiced*, …), open the documents of the contact.
* To get a file of the contacts select them in the list view, then **Actions
  ‣ Export**: choose the fields and the format (xlsx or csv).
* **Import records** in the :version:`16|**Favorites** menu;*|gear menu` of
  the list loads contacts from a file.

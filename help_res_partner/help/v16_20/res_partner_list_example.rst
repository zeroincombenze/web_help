.. _example:

Example
-------

**Contacts of a customer company**

Type the company name in the search bar and select *Search Related Company
for: …*: the list shows its people and addresses.

**Customers by country**

Choose the *Companies* filter, then **Group By ‣ Country**.

**Find duplicated contacts**

Switch to the list view, sort by name and look for equal names, or group by
Tax ID with *Add Custom Group*; merge them with **Actions ‣ Merge**.

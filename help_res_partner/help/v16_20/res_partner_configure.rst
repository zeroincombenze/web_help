.. _configure:

Configure
---------

Open **Contacts ‣ Configuration** (Administration rights):

* **Contact Tags**, **Contact Titles** and **Industries**: the values
  proposed in the contacts.
* **Localization**: **Countries**, **Fed. States** and **Country Group**;
  the country sets the address format and the Tax ID check.
* **Bank Accounts**: **Banks** and the **Bank Accounts** of the contacts.

In **Settings**, Invoicing section, *Verify VAT Numbers* checks the European
VAT numbers online (VIES); **Settings ‣ Languages** adds the languages for the
:field:`language <lang>` of the contacts.

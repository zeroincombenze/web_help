.. _report:

Report and export file
----------------------

* The contact has no print of its own: its name and address are printed on
  quotations, orders, deliveries and invoices, using the invoice or delivery
  address of the company when there is one.
* The smart buttons at the top right of the form open the documents of the
  contact: *Sales*, *Purchases*, *Invoiced*, *Vendor Bills*, meetings and
  opportunities, depending on the installed applications.
* The chatter at the bottom keeps the messages and the emails exchanged with
  the contact.
* To get a file of the contacts, select them in the list view, then **Action
  ‣ Export**: choose the fields and the format (xlsx or csv). See *Import and
  Export data* in the **Ready** page of the help.

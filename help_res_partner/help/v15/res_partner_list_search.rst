.. _search:

Suggestions for search
----------------------

* **Contacts** lists all the contacts, **Sales ‣ Orders ‣ Customers** the
  customers, :version:`11|**Purchases ‣ Purchase ‣ Vendors**;12|**Purchase ‣
  Purchase ‣ Vendors**;*|**Purchase ‣ Orders ‣ Vendors**` the vendors.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: the name (also the
  :field:`email <email>`, or the exact :field:`internal reference <ref>`),
  *Tag*, *Salesperson* or *Related Company* (the company and all its
  contacts).
* **Filters**: :version:`11|*Persons*;*|*Individuals*` or *Companies*;
  :version:`<=13|*Customers* or *Vendors*;*|*Customer Invoices* or *Vendor
  Bills*`\ :version:`<=12| (companies and individuals only, not the people of
  a company)`; *Archived* shows the archived contacts; *Add Custom Filter*
  builds any condition.
* **Group By**: *Salesperson*, *Company* or *Country*.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.

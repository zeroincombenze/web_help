.. _configure:

How to set up
-------------

* **Contacts ‣ Configuration**: *Contact Tags*, *Contact Titles* (Mr., Mrs.,
  …)\ :version:`>=14|, *Industries*`, *Localization* (countries, states,
  country groups) and *Bank Accounts* (banks and partner bank accounts).
* **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**: pricelists shown on the customer,
  :version:`11|*Warnings*;*|*Sale Order Warnings*` on the customer (**Internal
  Notes** tab).
* :version:`11|**Purchases**;*|**Purchase**` **‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**:
  warnings on the vendor.
* **Invoicing ‣ Configuration**: payment terms and fiscal positions proposed in
  the **Invoicing** tab; the default receivable and payable accounts come from
  the chart of accounts.
* Install the *base_vat* module to check the :field:`TIN <vat>`.

Update record
-------------

#. Open the contact, press **Edit**, change the fields and press **Save**
   (**Discard** drops the changes).
#. Changing the address of a company updates the address of all its contacts
   of type *Contact*; invoice, shipping and other addresses keep their own.
   The address of a *Contact* is changed on the company: press **(edit)**.
#. The commercial data (TIN, payment terms, fiscal position, pricelist,
   accounts) belongs to the company: changing it on the company changes it for
   all its contacts.
#. Changing the company type from *Individual* to *Company* lets the contact
   have its own contacts and addresses. When an individual carries a company
   name (e.g. from a website sign-up), **Create company** turns it into a new
   company.
#. Documents already created keep the address they were made with: change it
   on the document itself.
#. **Active** at the top right archives the contact: it is hidden from lists
   and selections but kept on the documents. Press **Archived** to restore it.
#. To delete a contact select it in the list and use **Action ‣ Delete**: a
   contact used by orders, invoices or journal entries cannot be deleted,
   archive it instead.

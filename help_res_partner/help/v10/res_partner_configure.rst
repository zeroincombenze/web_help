.. _configure:

Configure
---------

* **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Contacts** (developer mode): *Contact Tags*,
  *Contact Titles* (Mr., Mrs., …), *Localization* (countries, states,
  currencies) and *Bank Accounts* (banks and partner bank accounts).
* **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**: *pricelists* shown on the customer,
  *warnings* on the customer (**Internal Notes** tab).
* **Purchases ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**: warnings on the vendor.
* **Accounting ‣ Configuration**: payment terms and fiscal positions proposed
  in the **Accounting** tab; the default receivable and payable accounts come
  from the chart of accounts.
* Install the *base_vat* module to check the :field:`TIN <vat>`.

New record
----------

#. Open **Contacts**, or **Sales ‣ Sales ‣ Customers**, or **Purchases ‣
   Purchase ‣ Vendors**, and press **Create**.
#. Choose **Company** or **Individual** and type the :field:`name <name>`
   (required). For a person of a company, type the company in the
   :field:`company <parent_id>` field: the address is taken from the company.
#. Fill in the address, the :field:`TIN <vat>`, phone, mobile and
   :field:`email <email>`, the :field:`tags <category_id>` and the
   :field:`language <lang>`.
#. In the **Contacts & Addresses** tab of a company press **Create** to add a
   person or an address: choose *Contact*, *Invoice address*, *Shipping
   address* or *Other address*, then fill in its data and press **Save &
   Close**.
#. In the **Sales & Purchases** tab check :field:`Is a Customer <customer>`
   and/or :field:`Is a Vendor <supplier>` (set automatically by the menu you
   started from) and choose the :field:`salesperson <user_id>`.
#. With Invoicing installed, complete the **Accounting** tab: payment terms,
   fiscal position and bank accounts.
#. Press **Save**.

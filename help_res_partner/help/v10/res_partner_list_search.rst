.. _search:

How to search
-------------

* **Contacts** lists all the contacts, **Sales ‣ Sales ‣ Customers** starts
  with the *Customers* filter, **Purchases ‣ Purchase ‣ Vendors** with the
  *Vendors* filter.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: the name (also the
  :field:`email <email>`, or the exact :field:`internal reference <ref>`),
  *Tag*, *Salesperson* or *Related Company* (the company and all its
  contacts).
* **Filters**: *Persons* or *Companies*; *Customers* or *Vendors* (companies
  and individuals only, not the people of a company); *Archived* shows the
  archived contacts; *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: *Salesperson*, *Company* or *Country*.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.

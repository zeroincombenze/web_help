.. _example:

Example
-------

**Customers of a country**

Choose the *Customers* filter, then **Group By ‣ Country**: open the group of
the country.

**All the people of a company**

Type the company name in the search bar and select *Search Related Company
for: …*.

**Contacts with a tag**

Type the tag in the search bar and select *Search Tag for: …*; save it with
**Favorites ‣ Save current search** to use it again.

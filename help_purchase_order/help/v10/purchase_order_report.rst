Report
------

* **Request for Quotation**: the PDF of the RFQ.
  Press **Print RFQ** in the header, or use the **Print** menu above the form.
* **Purchase Order**: the PDF of the confirmed order, from the **Print** menu
  above the form, or selecting the orders in the list and using **Print**.
* **Send RFQ by Email** and **Send PO by Email** attach the PDF to the email;
  the sent message and its attachment stay in the chatter of the order.
* Purchase analysis: **Purchases ‣ Reports** (purchase managers) shows the
  ordered quantities and amounts by period, vendor, product or category, as
  graph or pivot.

How to search
-------------

* **Purchases ‣ Purchase ‣ Requests for Quotation** lists the RFQs,
  **Purchases ‣ Purchase ‣ Purchase Orders** the orders to approve and the
  confirmed ones, **Purchases ‣ Control ‣ Vendor Bills** the bills and
  **Purchases ‣ Control ‣ Incoming Products** the goods still to receive.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Reference* (order
  number), *Vendor*, *Product* or *Created by*.
* **Filters**: *Quotations*, *Purchase Orders* and *To Approve* narrow the
  list by status; *Waiting Bills* and *Bills Received* by
  :field:`billing status <invoice_status>`; *Unread Messages* shows the orders
  with new messages; *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: *Vendor*, *Order Date* or *Expected Date*, with subtotals of
  the untaxed amount and of the total.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.

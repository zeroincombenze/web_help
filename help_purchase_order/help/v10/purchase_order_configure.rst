Configure
---------

Open **Purchases ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**:

* **Logistics**: *Purchase Lead Time*, the safety days added to the vendor
  lead time; *Purchase Order Modification*, to lock the confirmed orders;
  *Levels of Approvals*, one step or 2 levels of approval for the orders over
  the *Double validation amount*.
* **Purchase Order**: product variants, units of measure, costing method,
  *call for tenders* to get quotes from several vendors, *vendor pricelists*
  (menu **Purchases ‣ Purchase ‣ Vendor Pricelists**) and warnings on products
  and vendors.
* **Location & Warehouse**: dropshipping, the vendor delivers directly to your
  customer.

On each product, the **Purchase** tab holds the vendors with their price and
delivery lead time, and the *Control Purchase Bills* policy: *On ordered
quantities* or *On received quantities* (default). Analytic accounts on
purchase lines are enabled in **Accounting ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**,
payment terms are managed in **Accounting ‣ Configuration ‣ Payment Terms**.

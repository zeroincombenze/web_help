Example
-------

**Orders of a vendor still to bill**

Choose the *Waiting Bills* filter, type the vendor name in the search bar and
select *Search Vendor for: …*; the total is shown at the bottom of the list.

**Monthly purchases of each vendor**

Choose the *Purchase Orders* filter, then **Group By ‣ Vendor** and
**Group By ‣ Order Date**: each group shows its subtotal.

**Print several orders**

Select the orders with the check boxes, then **Print ‣ Purchase Order**.

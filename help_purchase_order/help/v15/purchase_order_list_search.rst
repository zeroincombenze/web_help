.. _search:

Suggestions for search
----------------------

.. help-version:: <=12

   * In the :version:`11|**Purchases**;*|**Purchase**` app, **Purchase ‣
     Requests for Quotation** lists the RFQs, **Purchase ‣ Purchase Orders**
     the orders to approve and the confirmed ones, **Control ‣ Vendor Bills**
     the bills and **Control ‣ Incoming Products** the goods still to
     receive.

.. help-version:: >=13

   * **Purchase ‣ Orders ‣ Requests for Quotation** lists the RFQs, **Purchase
     ‣ Orders ‣ Purchase Orders** the orders to approve and the confirmed ones;
     the vendor bills are in **Invoicing ‣ Vendors ‣ Bills**.

* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Order Reference*,
  *Vendor* or *Product*.
* **Filters**: :version:`>=12|*My Purchases* shows your orders only,`
  :version:`<=12|*Quotations*;*|*RFQs*`, *Purchase Orders* and *To Approve*
  narrow the list by status; *Waiting Bills* and *Bills Received* by
  :field:`billing status <invoice_status>`; *Add Custom Filter* builds any
  condition.
* **Group By**: *Vendor*,\ :version:`>=12| *Purchase Representative*,` *Order
  Date*\ :version:`<=13| or *Expected Date*`, with subtotals of the untaxed
  amount and of the total.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.

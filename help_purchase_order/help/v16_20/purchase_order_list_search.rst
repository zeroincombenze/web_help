How to search
-------------

* **Purchase ‣ Orders ‣ Requests for Quotation** lists the RFQs, **Purchase ‣
  Orders ‣ Purchase Orders** the confirmed orders.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Order* (reference or
  vendor reference), *Vendor*, *Buyer* or *Product*. Several criteria are
  combined; remove one with its ✕.
* **Filters**: *My Purchases* and *My Orders* show your documents only;
  *Starred* the orders marked with the star; *RFQs*, *Purchase Orders* and *To
  Approve* narrow the list by status; *Draft RFQs*, *Waiting RFQs* and *Late
  RFQs* (order deadline passed) follow the requests; *Waiting Bills* and
  *Bills Received* follow the billing; *Order Date* selects a period; *Add
  Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: *Vendor*, *Buyer* or *Order Date*, with subtotals; *Add Custom
  Group* groups by any field, e.g. :field:`billing status <invoice_status>`
  or status.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.
* Optional columns are shown or hidden from the menu at the right of the column
  headers; the kanban, calendar, pivot and graph views are at the top right.

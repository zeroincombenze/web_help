.. _configure:

Configure
---------

Open **Purchase ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**:

* **Orders**: *Purchase Order Approval* (orders above a *Minimum Amount* must
  be approved by a purchase manager), *Lock Confirmed Orders*, *Warnings* on
  products and vendors, *Purchase Agreements* (blanket orders and calls for
  tenders), *Receipt Reminder*.
* **Invoicing**: the default *Bill Control*, *Ordered quantities* or *Received
  quantities*; each product can override it in its **Control Policy** field.
  *3-way matching* allows paying only bills of received goods.
* **Products**: *Variants*, *Variant Grid Entry*, *Product Packagings* and
  *Units of Measure*.

Vendor prices and lead times are managed in **Purchase ‣ Configuration ‣
Vendor Pricelists** or in the **Purchase** tab of the product; payment terms
in **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Payment Terms**.

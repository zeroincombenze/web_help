.. _example:

Example
-------

**Orders of a vendor still to bill**

Choose the *Waiting Bills* filter, type the vendor name in the search bar and
select *Search Vendor for: …*; the total is shown at the bottom of the list.

**Monthly purchases of each buyer**

Choose the *Purchase Orders* filter, then **Group By ‣ Buyer** and
**Group By ‣ Order Date ‣ Month**: each group shows its subtotal.

**Bill several orders at once**

Select the confirmed orders with the check boxes and press **Create Bills**.

.. help-version:: >=18

   **Merge the RFQs of the same vendor**

   Select the RFQs with the check boxes, then **Actions ‣ Merge**: the lines
   are moved to the oldest RFQ and the others are cancelled. Vendor, currency
   and destination must be the same.

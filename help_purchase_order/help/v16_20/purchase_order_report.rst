.. _report:

Report
------

* **Request for Quotation** and **Purchase Order**: the PDF of the RFQ or of
  the confirmed order. Press **Print RFQ** in the header, or print it from the
  **Print** menu\ :version:`>=17| (under the gear icon next to the record
  name)`, or from the list after selecting the orders.
* **Send by Email** and **Send PO by Email** attach the same PDF to the email;
  the sent message and its attachment stay in the chatter of the order. The
  vendor can see the order in the portal.
* Purchase analysis: **Purchase ‣ Reporting ‣ Purchase** shows the ordered,
  received and billed quantities and amounts by period, vendor, buyer or
  product, as graph or pivot.

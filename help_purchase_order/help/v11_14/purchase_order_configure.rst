.. _configure:

How to set up
-------------

Open :version:`11|**Purchases**;*|**Purchase**` **‣ Configuration ‣
Settings**:

* **Orders**: :version:`11|*Order Approval*;*|*Purchase Order Approval*`, the
  orders over the *Minimum Amount* need the approval of a manager; *Lock
  Confirmed Orders*; *Purchase Agreements* (calls for tenders and blanket
  orders); :version:`11|*Warnings*;*|*Purchase Warnings*` on products and
  vendors\ :version:`>=14|, and *Receipt Reminder*, to ask the vendor to
  confirm the receipt date`.
* **Invoicing**: *Bill Control*, ordered or received quantities, the default of
  the *Control Policy* of the products; *3-way matching* of orders, receipts
  and bills.
* **Products**: variants, units of measure\ :version:`11-12|, *Vendor
  Pricelists*;*|, *Purchase Grid Entry*`.
* *Dropshipping*, the vendor delivers directly to your customer, and
  :version:`11|the purchase lead time;*|*Security Lead Time for Purchase*`,
  the safety days added to the vendor lead time.

On each product, the **Purchase** tab holds the vendors with their price and
delivery lead time, and the *Control Policy*: *On ordered quantities* or *On
received quantities*. Payment terms are managed in **Invoicing ‣ Configuration
‣** :version:`<13|**Management**;*|**Invoicing**` **‣ Payment Terms**.

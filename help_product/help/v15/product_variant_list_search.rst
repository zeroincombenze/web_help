.. _search:

Suggestions for search
----------------------

* With the variants setting, :version:`11|**Sales ‣ Catalog ‣ Product
  Variants**;*|**Sales ‣ Products ‣ Product Variants**` and the same menu of
  Purchase and Inventory list the variants.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Product* (name, internal
  reference or barcode), *Product Category*, *Attribute Values* or *Product
  Template* (all the variants of a product).
* **Filters**: *Services* or *Products*; *Can be Sold*, *Can be Purchased*;
  *Archived*; *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: e.g. by *Product Template* (a custom group) to see the
  variants of each product together.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.

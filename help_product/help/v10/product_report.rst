.. _report:

Report and export file
----------------------

* **Print ‣ Products Labels**: labels with name, reference, barcode and price.
  Select the products in the list, or open one, and use **Print**.
* **Print ‣ Pricelist** (product variants): the prices of the selected
  products in a pricelist, for the quantities you choose.
* The stat buttons at the top right open the related data: **On Hand**,
  **Forecasted**, **Traceability**, **Routes**, **Sales**, **Purchases** and
  **Variants**, depending on the installed applications.
* To get a file of the products, select them in the list view, then
  :version:`<=16|**Action ‣ Export**;*|**Actions ‣ Export**`: choose the
  fields and the format (xlsx or csv). See *Import and Export data* in the
  **Ready** page of the help.

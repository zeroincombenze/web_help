.. _example:

Example
-------

**Products out of stock**

Choose the *Exhausted Stock* filter (Inventory installed): the stockable
products with no quantity on hand.

**Prices of a pricelist**

Type the pricelist name in the search bar and select *Search Pricelist for:
…*: the price shown on each product is the one of that pricelist.

**Print the labels of some products**

Select the products with the check boxes, then **Print ‣ Products Labels**.

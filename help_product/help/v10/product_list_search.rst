.. _search:

How to search
-------------

* **Sales ‣ Sales ‣ Products** starts with the *Can be Sold* filter,
  **Purchases ‣ Purchase ‣ Products** with *Can be Purchased*, **Inventory ‣
  Inventory Control ‣ Products** shows all the products.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Product* (name, internal
  reference or barcode), *Product Category* (with its subcategories),
  *Attributes* or *Pricelist* (shows the prices of that pricelist).
* **Filters**: *Services* or *Products*; *Can be Sold*, *Can be Purchased*;
  with Inventory *Available Products*, *Exhausted Stock*, *Negative Stock*;
  *Archived* shows the archived products; *Add Custom Filter* builds any
  condition.
* **Group By ‣ Add custom group**: e.g. by *Internal Category* or *Product
  Type*.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.

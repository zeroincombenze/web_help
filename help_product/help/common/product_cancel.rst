How to archive or delete record
-------------------------------

#. A product no longer used is archived, with all its variants: it is no
   longer proposed on new documents but stays on the old ones.
   :version:`<=12|In the form press [\`Active\`] at the top right;<=16|In the
   form use **Action ‣ Archive**;*|In the form use the gear icon ‣
   **Archive**`, or select the products in the list and use
   :version:`<=16|**Action ‣ Archive**;*|**Actions ‣ Archive**`.\ :version:`>=20|
   A product that is part of a combo cannot be archived: remove it from the
   combos first.`
#. The *Archived* filter shows the archived products;
   :version:`<=12|open one and press [\`Archived\`] to restore it;<=16|select
   them and use **Action ‣ Unarchive** to restore them;*|select them and use
   **Actions ‣ Unarchive** to restore them`.
#. To delete a product, open it and use :version:`<17|**Action ‣
   Delete**;*|the gear icon ‣ **Delete**`, or select the products in the list
   and use :version:`<=16|**Action ‣ Delete**;*|**Actions ‣ Delete**`: its
   variants are deleted too. A product used by orders, invoices or stock
   moves cannot be deleted: archive it instead.

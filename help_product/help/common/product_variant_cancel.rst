How to archive or delete record
-------------------------------

#. A variant no longer sold is archived alone: the product and its other
   variants stay active. :version:`<=12|In the form press [\`Active\`] at the
   top right;<=16|In the form use **Action ‣ Archive**;*|In the form use the
   gear icon ‣ **Archive**`, or select the variants in the list and use
   :version:`<=16|**Action ‣ Archive**;*|**Actions ‣ Archive**`.
#. The *Archived* filter shows the archived variants;
   :version:`<=12|open one and press [\`Archived\`] to restore it;<=16|select
   them and use **Action ‣ Unarchive** to restore them;*|select them and use
   **Actions ‣ Unarchive** to restore them`.
#. To delete a variant, open it and use :version:`<17|**Action ‣
   Delete**;*|the gear icon ‣ **Delete**`, or select the variants in the list
   and use :version:`<=16|**Action ‣ Delete**;*|**Actions ‣ Delete**`.
   Deleting the last variant of a product deletes the product too. A variant
   used by orders, invoices or stock moves cannot be deleted: archive it
   instead.

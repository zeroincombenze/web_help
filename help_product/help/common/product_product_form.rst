Product variant
===============

.. _description:

Description
-----------

A product variant is one version of a product, e.g. the *T-shirt* in size
*M* and color *Blue*. It is made by the :field:`attribute values
<product_template_variant_value_ids>` of the product and has its own
:field:`internal reference <default_code>`, :field:`barcode <barcode>`, stock
and, if set, an extra price. All the other data belong to the
:field:`product <product_tmpl_id>` and are shared by its variants. A product
without attributes has exactly one variant.

.. _faq:

FAQ
---

.. help-question:: Why can't I create a variant with New?

   Variants are generated by the attributes of the product: add the value in
   the **Attributes & Variants** tab of the product.

.. help-question:: Why does a change to the variant change all the others?

   Name, prices, category, taxes and the other common data belong to the
   product: only reference, barcode, extra price, weight and volume are per
   variant.

.. help-question:: Why can't I delete the variant?

   A variant used in documents cannot be deleted: archive it, or remove its
   value from the product attributes (the variant is then archived).

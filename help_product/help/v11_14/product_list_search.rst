.. _search:

Suggestions for search
----------------------

* :version:`11|**Sales ‣ Catalog ‣ Products**;*|**Sales ‣ Products ‣
  Products**` starts with the *Can be Sold* filter, the products menu of the
  purchase app with *Can be Purchased*, :version:`<=13|**Inventory ‣ Master
  Data ‣ Products**;*|**Inventory ‣ Products ‣ Products**` shows all the
  products.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Product* (name, internal
  reference or barcode), *Product Category* (with its subcategories),
  *Attributes* or *Pricelist* (shows the prices of that pricelist).
* **Filters**: *Services* or *Products*; *Can be Sold*, *Can be Purchased*;
  with Inventory *Available Products*, :version:`11|*Exhausted Stock*,
  *Negative Stock*;*|*Negative Forecasted Quantity*`; *Archived* shows the
  archived products; *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: :version:`<=12|**Add custom group**, e.g. by *Internal
  Category* or *Product Type*;*|*Product Type* or *Product Category*`.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.

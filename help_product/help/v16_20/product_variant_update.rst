.. _update_record:

Update record
-------------

#. Open the variant and change reference, barcode, cost, weight or volume;
   the record is saved when you leave it, or press the cloud icon.
#. To change the extra price of a value open the product, tab **Attributes &
   Variants**, press **Configure** on the attribute line and set the extra
   price: it applies to every variant with that value.
#. **Actions ‣ Archive** hides a variant no longer sold; the product and its
   other variants stay active.
#. Removing a value from the product attributes archives the variants using
   it; adding it back restores them.
#. A variant used in documents cannot be deleted: archive it instead.

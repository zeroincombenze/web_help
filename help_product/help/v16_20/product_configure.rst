.. _configure:

Configure
---------

* **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings** (or Inventory, Purchase) section
  **Product Catalog** / **Products**: *Variants*, *Units of Measure*,
  *Pricelists*\ :version:`>=17| and the *Variant Grid Entry* of orders`.
* **Sales ‣ Configuration ‣ Products**: **Attributes** and their values (with
  the extra price of each value), **Product Categories**, **Product Tags**\
  :version:`>=18|, **Combo Choices**`.
* **Inventory ‣ Configuration ‣ Product Categories**: accounts, costing method
  and removal strategy of each category; **Units of Measure** and their
  categories in the same menu.
* **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**: the default sales and purchase
  taxes proposed on new products.

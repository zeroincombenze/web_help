How to search
-------------

* **Sales ‣ Products ‣ Products**, **Purchase ‣ Products ‣ Products** and
  **Inventory ‣ Products ‣ Products** list the same products.
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: *Product* (name, internal
  reference or barcode), *Product Category*, *Attributes*,
  …. Several criteria are combined; remove one with its ✕.
* **Filters**: *Services* and :version:`<=17|*Products*;*|*Goods*`;
  :version:`<=17|*Can be Sold* and *Can be Purchased*;*|*Sales* and
  *Purchase*`; *Archived* shows the archived products; *Add Custom Filter*
  builds any condition.
* **Group By**: *Product Type* or *Product Category*; *Add Custom Group*
  groups by any field.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.

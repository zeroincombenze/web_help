.. _example:

Example
-------

**A T-shirt sold in 3 sizes and 2 colors**

#. Activate the *Variants* setting.
#. Create the product *T-shirt*, tab **Attributes & Variants**: add *Size*
   with *S*, *M*, *L* and *Color* with *White*, *Blue*.
#. Odoo creates 6 variants; the **Variants** smart button lists them to set
   reference and barcode of each one.
#. To sell *L* for 2 more, press **Configure** on the *Size* line and set the
   extra price of *L* to 2.

**A service invoiced by hours**

Create a product of type *Service*, unit of measure *Hours*, and set its
price per hour.

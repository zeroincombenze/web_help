.. _report:

Report
------

* **Print Labels** (in the header of the form, or in the list after selecting
  the products): choose the quantity and the layout to print price and barcode
  labels.
* With the *Pricelists* setting, select the products and use
  :version:`<=17|**Actions ‣ Generate Pricelist Report**;*|**Actions ‣
  Pricelist Report**` to print their prices for a pricelist and quantities.
* The smart buttons at the top of the form (*Sold*, *Purchased*, *On Hand*,
  …, added by the installed apps) open the documents and the stock of the
  product.

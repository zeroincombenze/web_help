.. _example:

Example
-------

**Change the price of several products**

Switch to the list view, select the products, click the *Sales Price* cell of
one of them, type the new price and confirm: all the selected products get it.

**Products of a category**

Type the category in the search bar and select *Search Product Category for:
…*, or **Group By ‣ Product Category**.

**Print the labels of new products**

Select the products in the list and press **Print Labels**.

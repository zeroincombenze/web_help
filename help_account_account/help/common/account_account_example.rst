.. _example:

Example
-------

**A new expense account with its default tax.** Open **Invoicing ‣
Configuration ‣ Accounting ‣ Chart of Accounts** and press [`New`]. Type the
code ``600100``, the account name *Office supplies* and the type *Expenses*,
and save the line. Press the arrow button at the end of the line, in the
**Accounting** tab fill :field:`Default Taxes <tax_ids>` with a purchase tax
and leave the form. When you choose this account on a vendor bill line
without a product, the tax is proposed.

How to set up
-------------

* **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Accounting ‣ Journals** (*Billing Manager*
  only): every journal is a card of the dashboard; the cards follow the order
  of this list, which can be changed by dragging the rows.
* In the journal form: the :field:`Journal Name <name>` and the
  :field:`Type <type>` shown on the card; in the *Journal Entries* tab the
  :field:`Short Code <code>` and the :field:`Default Debit Account
  <default_debit_account_id>` and :field:`Default Credit Account
  <default_credit_account_id>`, whose balance is the *Balance in General
  Ledger* of a bank or cash card.
* A bank journal has the *Bank Account* tab: the bank account, the bank and
  the :field:`Bank Feeds <bank_statements_source>`, which decides whether the
  card shows **Configure**.
* **⋮ ‣ Settings** on a card opens the same form; **⋮ ‣ Add to Favorites** /
  **Remove from Favorites** adds the journal to the *Favorites* filter or
  removes it.
* The access right of each user is in **Settings ‣ Users & Companies ‣
  Users**: field *Accounting & Finance* (*Billing* or *Billing Manager*) and,
  in developer mode, *Show Full Accounting Features* in the *Technical
  Settings* section, needed to see the dashboard.

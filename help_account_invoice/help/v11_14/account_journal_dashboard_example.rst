.. _example:

Example
-------

**Following up the unpaid invoices**

On the card of the sales journal click *N Unpaid Invoices*: the list shows
the validated invoices not paid yet. Add the *Overdue* filter to keep only
those whose due date is passed.

**Recording a bank statement by hand**

On the card of the bank journal click **Create** (*Create Statements*),
write the *Starting Balance* and the *Ending Balance* and, in the
*Transactions* tab, a line for each movement of the paper statement, with
its partner, then save. Back on the dashboard press **Reconcile N Items** and
match each line with its invoice or payment.

**Paying a vendor without a bill**

On the card of the bank journal open **⋮ ‣ New ‣ Vendor Payment**: the
payment is ready with *Send Money* and this journal.

**Keeping the dashboard short**

As *Billing Manager*, open **⋮** on a journal you rarely use and click
**Remove from Favorites**: the journal leaves the *Favorites* filter, with
which the dashboard opens. Remove the filter to find it again.

.. _example:

Example
-------

**Following up the late invoices**

On the card of the sales journal click *N Late*: the list shows the posted
invoices not fully paid whose due date is passed. Open one and use **Send**
to remind the customer, or **Pay** when the money has arrived.

**Creating a bill from its PDF**

On the card of the purchase journal press **Upload** and choose the PDF of
the bill, or drop the file on the card: a draft bill is created with the
file attached. Check the vendor, the lines and the amounts, then
**Confirm**.

**Paying a vendor without a bill**

On the card of the bank journal open **⋮ ‣ New ‣ Vendor Payment**: the
payment is ready with this journal. Choose the vendor and the amount, then
**Confirm**.

**Keeping the dashboard short**

As *Administrator*, open **⋮** on a journal you rarely use and click **☆**:
the card leaves the dashboard, which shows only the favorite journals.
Remove the *Favorites* filter to find it again.

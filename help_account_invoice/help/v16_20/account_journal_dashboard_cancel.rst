How to archive or delete record
-------------------------------

* The cards are journals: they are archived or deleted from
  **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Accounting ‣ Journals** (or
  **Accounting ‣ Configuration ‣ Accounting ‣ Journals**, *Administrator*
  only), not from the dashboard.
* To hide a card without touching the journal, remove its **☆**: the
  journal is still used, it only leaves the *Favorites* filter.
* A journal no longer used is archived: open it and use
  :version:`<17|**Action ‣ Archive**;*|the gear icon ‣ **Archive**`, or
  select it in the list and use :version:`<17|**Action ‣
  Archive**;*|**Actions ‣ Archive**`. Its card leaves the dashboard and the
  journal is no longer proposed on new documents; its entries are kept. The
  *Archived* filter of the journal list finds it, and [`Unarchive`] restores
  it.
* Only a journal without journal entries can be deleted, with
  :version:`<17|**Action ‣ Delete**;*|**Actions ‣ Delete**` in the journal
  list; a journal with entries is archived instead.

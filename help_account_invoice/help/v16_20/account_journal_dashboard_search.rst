.. _search:

Suggestions for search
----------------------

* The dashboard opens with the *Favorites* filter: only the journals marked
  with the ★ star of the **⋮** menu. Remove it with its ✕ to see every
  journal of the selected companies. Do not mix it up with the *Favorites*
  section of the search panel, which keeps your saved searches.
* Type in the search bar to find a *Journal* by name or short code.
* **Filters** on the type: *Sales*, *Purchases*, *Liquidity* (bank, cash and
  credit card), *Miscellaneous*; *Archived* shows the archived journals.
  *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search**, in the search panel, keeps the filters
  for later; tick *Default filter* to open the dashboard with them.

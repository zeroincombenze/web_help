.. _search:

Suggestions for search
----------------------

* :version:`<12|**Invoicing ‣ Purchases ‣ Vendor Bills**;*|**Invoicing ‣
  Vendors ‣ Bills**` lists the vendor bills.
  :version:`>=11|The vendor credit notes have their own menu next to it.`
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: 
  :version:`10|the number (it also searches the source document, the vendor
  reference and the partner), the vendor, the period or the journal;*|the
  number, the vendor, the vendor reference, the product or the journal`.
  Several criteria are combined; remove one with its ✕.
* **Filters** on the status:
  :version:`10|*Draft*, *Proforma*, *Not Paid*, and *Invoices* / *Refunds*
  for the type;11|*Draft*, *Open*, *Paid*;12|*Draft*, *Open*, *In Payment*,
  *Paid*;13|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *Not Paid*, *In Payment*,
  *Paid*;14|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *Not Paid*, *Partially Paid*, *In
  Payment*, *Paid*, *Reversed*;15|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *Unpaid*,
  *Paid*;16-17|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *Unpaid*, *Paid*,
  *Reversed*;*|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *To pay*, *In payment*, and
  *Bills* / *Refunds* for the type`. *Overdue* shows the unpaid bills whose
  due date is passed\
  :version:`10|.;<18|. *Invoice Date* and *Due Date* select a period.;*|. *Bill
  Date* and *Due Date* select a period.`
  :version:`>=14|*To Check* shows the bills to review.`
  *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: :version:`<13|*Partner*, *Status*;13-14|*Status*;*|*Partner*,
  *Status*, *Journal*`, :version:`<18|*Invoice Date*;*|*Bill Date*` or *Due
  Date* (year, quarter, month, week, day), with subtotals.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.
* The kanban, pivot and graph views are at the top right, when available.

.. _configure:

Configure
---------

.. help-version:: <13

   **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**:

   * *Default Taxes* proposed on the lines, and the *Rounding Method* of the
     taxes (per line or globally).
   * *Multi-Currencies*: invoices in foreign currencies.
   * *Warnings*: a message shown when a partner or a product is chosen.
   * :version:`10|*Allow pro-forma invoices*: the **PRO-FORMA** button.;11|*Cash
     Rounding*: the rounding of the total, e.g. to 0.05.;12|*Cash Rounding*,
     and the *Default Sending Options* (print, email) of **Send & Print**.`

   Other menus:

   * **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Management ‣ Payment Terms**: the due
     dates, e.g. *30 days end of month* or 30% now and 70% in 60 days.
   * **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Accounting ‣ Journals**: the sales and
     purchase journals and their number sequence. *Allow Cancelling Entries*
     (module *Cancel Journal Entries*) enables **Cancel Invoice**.
   * **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Accounting ‣ Taxes** and **Fiscal
     Positions**.
   * On the partner, **Invoicing** tab (*Accounting* tab for an accountant):
     payment terms, fiscal position and receivable / payable accounts proposed
     on the invoices.

.. help-version:: >=13

   **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Settings**:

   * *Default Taxes* proposed on the lines, and the *Rounding Method* of the
     taxes (per line or globally).
   * *Currencies*: invoices in foreign currencies.
   * *Customer Invoices*: *Warnings* on partners and products, *Cash
     Rounding*\ :version:`>=14|, *Default Incoterm* and *Default Terms &
     Conditions* printed on the invoices`\ :version:`14-17|, *Default Sending
     Options* (print, email) of **Send & Print**`\ :version:`>=16|, *Customer
     Addresses* (delivery address on the invoice)`.
   * *Customer Payments*: :version:`<16|*SEPA QR Code*;*|*QR Codes*` on the
     invoices, so that the customer pays from the banking app.

   Other menus:

   * **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Invoicing ‣ Payment Terms**: the due dates,
     e.g. *30 days end of month* or 30% now and 70% in 60 days.
   * **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Accounting ‣ Journals**: the sales and
     purchase journals and their number sequence;
     :version:`<18|*Lock Posted Entries with Hash*;*|*Secure Posted Entries
     with Hash*` secures the posted invoices, which can no longer be reset to
     draft.
   * **Invoicing ‣ Configuration ‣ Accounting ‣ Taxes** and **Fiscal
     Positions**.
   * On the partner, **Invoicing** tab (*Accounting* tab for an accountant):
     payment terms, fiscal position and receivable / payable accounts proposed
     on the invoices.

   .. help-version:: >=18

      The sending method of each customer (email, post, electronic invoice) is
      set on the partner, **Invoicing** tab, *Invoice sending*.

.. _search:

How to search
-------------

* :version:`<12|**Invoicing ‣ Sales ‣ Customer Invoices**;*|**Invoicing ‣
  Customers ‣ Invoices**` lists the customer invoices,
  :version:`<12|**Invoicing ‣ Purchases ‣ Vendor Bills**;*|**Invoicing ‣
  Vendors ‣ Bills**` the vendor bills.
  :version:`11|Credit notes have their own menus next to them.;>=12|Credit
  notes and refunds have their own menus next to them.`
* Type in the search bar and choose what to search: the number, the partner,
  the salesperson, the product or the journal. Several criteria are combined;
  remove one with its ✕.
* **Filters** on the status:
  :version:`10|*Draft*, *Proforma*, *Not Paid*, and *Invoices* / *Refunds*
  for the type;11|*Draft*, *Open*, *Paid*;12|*Draft*, *Open*, *In Payment*,
  *Paid*;13|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *Not Paid*, *In Payment*,
  *Paid*;14|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *Not Paid*, *Partially Paid*, *In
  Payment*, *Paid*, *Reversed*;15|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *Unpaid*,
  *Paid*;16-17|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *Unpaid*, *Paid*,
  *Reversed*;*|*Draft*, *Posted*, *Cancelled*, *To pay*, *In payment*, *Not
  Sent*, and *Invoices* / *Credit Notes* for the type`. *Overdue* shows the
  unpaid invoices whose due date is passed; *Invoice Date* and *Due Date*
  select a period.
  :version:`>=14|*To Check* shows the invoices to review.`
  *Add Custom Filter* builds any condition.
* **Group By**: :version:`<13|*Partner*, *Salesperson*, *Status*;13-14|*Salesperson*,
  *Status*;*|*Salesperson*, *Partner*, *Status*, *Journal*`, *Invoice Date*
  or *Due Date* (year, quarter, month, week, day), with subtotals.
* **Favorites ‣ Save current search** keeps filters and groups for later.
* The kanban, pivot and graph views are at the top right, when available.

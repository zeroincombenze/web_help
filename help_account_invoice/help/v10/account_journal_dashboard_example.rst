.. _example:

Example
-------

**Following up the unpaid invoices**

On the card of the sales journal click *Awaiting payments*: the list shows
the validated invoices not paid yet. Choose the *Overdue* filter to keep only
those whose due date is passed.

**Recording a bank statement by hand**

On the card of the bank journal press **New Statement**, write the starting
and ending balance and a line for each movement of the paper statement, with
its partner, then save. Back on the dashboard press **Reconcile N Items** and
match each line with its invoice or payment.

**Paying a vendor without a bill**

On the card of the bank journal open **More ‣ New ‣ Send Money** and press
**Create**: the payment is ready with *Send Money* and this journal.

**Keeping the dashboard short**

As *Adviser*, open **More** on a journal you rarely use and click **☆
Favorite**: the card leaves the dashboard, which shows only the favorite
journals. Remove the *Favorites* filter to find it again.
